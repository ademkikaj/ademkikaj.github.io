Hi, I am Adem, a **Senior Research Engineer at [Faktion](https://www.faktion.com)** and a **PhD student at [KU Leuven](https://www.kuleuven.be/english/kuleuven/)**.

At Faktion, I work across a range of **applied AI projects**, bringing advanced machine learning methods from research into practical, production-ready solutions for businesses. More broadly, I am interested in **translating ideas from academic AI research into practical systems used in industry**.

My research focuses on the **integration of graph neural networks with probabilistic logic programming**, at the intersection of **graph machine learning, (probabilistic) logic programming, and neurosymbolic AI**.

My PhD is part of the [iBOF](https://ai.kuleuven.be/news/luc-de-raedt-jesse-davis-ku-leuven-and-tijl-de-bie-ugent-one-of-the-first-15-ibof-projects) project under the supervision of [Prof. Luc De Raedt](https://wms.cs.kuleuven.be/people/lucderaedt/) and [Asst. Prof. Giuseppe Marra](https://www.giuseppemarra.com/). I previously worked as a **researcher at the [DTAI](https://dtai.cs.kuleuven.be/) research group** at KU Leuven.

Before that, I was a research member at the [Knowledge Technologies](https://kt.ijs.si/) department at the [Jožef Stefan Institute](https://ijs.si/ijsw/V001/JSI), where I also obtained my MSc working on **decision-support systems**.
